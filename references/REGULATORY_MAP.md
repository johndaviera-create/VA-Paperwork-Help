# Regulatory lookup map (navigation only — not legal conclusions)

Check applicable exact provisions; not every rule applies to every claim.

- 38 CFR § 3.303: principles relating to service connection
- 38 CFR § 3.304: additional direct-service-connection considerations
- 38 CFR § 3.306: aggravation of pre-service disability
- 38 CFR §§ 3.307, 3.309: relevant presumptive conditions and prerequisites
- 38 CFR § 3.310: secondary causation/aggravation
- 38 CFR § 3.159: evidence and duty-to-assist framework
- 38 CFR § 3.102: reasonable doubt
- 38 CFR § 3.2500: review options, continuous pursuit where applicable
- 38 CFR § 3.400: effective dates; verify exceptions and historical procedural context

- 38 CFR § 4.16: TDIU; case-specific adjudication only
- 38 CFR § 3.350 and 38 U.S.C. § 1114: SMC where applicable

## Workflow cautions

- An ICD-10 diagnosis code is not automatically a VA diagnostic code.
- The VA's chosen DC may be analogous, hyphenated, or affected by code-specific notes.
- Neither diagnosis nor symptom count implies service connection or a particular rating.
- Avoid claiming that two diagnoses should receive separate compensation for the *same manifestations*.
- Do not apply payment charts to decide disability percentages: monetary amounts and disability evaluation are different tasks.
- For review/appeal paths and deadlines, read the DECISION LETTER and official VA procedural instructions first.
