## 3. Potential discrepancies or areas for further review

| Question | Why it may matter | Records needed to resolve | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|

Include conflicting decision dates, earlier denial versus later grant, a diagnostic finding versus later adjudication, copied boilerplate about the wrong condition, and P&T/DEA date versus schedular 100% date when found. Never call a fact a "VA error" solely because an alternative evaluation seems possible.

## 4. Combined rating (optional)

List awarded individual ratings ONLY with furnished source, note whether bilateral factors apply, and include a verified official table calculation; otherwise `NOT COMPUTED` and reasons. Proposed new ratings should be separate hypotheticals with conspicuous labeling.

## 5. Sources and options

- Regulations and official guidance, each linked, dated, and tied to the actual finding.
- Free VA-accredited VSO locator: https://www.va.gov/get-help-from-accredited-representative/
- Decision reviews: https://www.va.gov/decision-reviews/ ; choose pathways only with decision-specific facts.

**Closing:** The veteran should check facts against the originals. The VA makes the decision, and clinicians supply independent medical findings.
