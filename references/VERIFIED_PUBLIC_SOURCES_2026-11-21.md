## Legal freshness failure examples

- A webpage saying the **February 17, 2026** medication rule remains current fails: it was rescinded **February 27, 2026**.
- A **proposed** mental-health rating schedule is not the operative §4.130 formula without a valid final/effective rule.
- An old award date may require point-in-time regulations; do not apply today's text retroactively by default.
- A BVA decision for another veteran is not a medical nexus opinion for the current veteran and is generally nonprecedential under §20.1303.
