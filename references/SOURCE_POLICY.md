# Official-source and version policy

## Priority hierarchy

1. **Controlling law:** 38 U.S.C. and operative 38 CFR (official Federal Register / govinfo, and eCFR's current or historical compiled text). eCFR is authoritative but unofficial; verify publication/effective dates in the Federal Register when a legal change is material.
2. **Official VA operational references:** VA's public disability pages and DBQs; VA's M21-1 (KnowVA) adjudication manual when relevant. These aid interpretation and processing but are not substitutes for controlling statutes/regulations.
3. **Case evidence:** Only veteran-supplied records, with provenance and page citations. Never use public web sources to establish personal medical history.
4. **Nonofficial material:** Useful only for discovering citations to verify independently. Never cite a marketing blog as sole authority for criteria.

## Required fields for every legal-source use

- Exact regulation section and diagnostic code, if applicable.
- Official URL (not just a page title).
- Date you accessed it; eCFR content "up to date as of" date (if displayed).
- Final rule effective date and history if materially changed.
- Specific rating criteria applied, including notes/qualifiers.
- Any historical-period uncertainty or pending change.

**Offline rule:** No current percentage/tier determinations when the governing text cannot be verified live in the environment. Permit evidence indexing and clearly labeled historical-only, non-current discussion.

## Specific version-control regression warning

VA changed § 4.10 with an interim rule on February 17, 2026, then RESCINDED that interim rule effective February 27, 2026, restoring prior text. **Do not use the rescinded rule as current law.** Verify against current eCFR and the official Feb. 27 final rule: https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-02-27/pdf/FR-2026-02-27.pdf

This example exists to test freshness; it does not excuse checking all other rules at every review. No regulation excerpts embedded in this skill should be treated as live criteria.
