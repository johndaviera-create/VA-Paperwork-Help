# Disclaimer — VA Disability Evidence Review

**This is independent, free, open-source educational software. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, certified by, or endorsed by the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs.**

## Private testing and independent review

The author privately tested earlier versions of this skill against their own VA medical records and claims decisions. The results informed safeguards for identity separation, adjudication chronology, accurate sourcing, and conservative interpretation. **No actual personal medical data, claim documents, identifiers, or derived real-case test fixtures are distributed with this project.**

This private testing is **not** an independent review, scientific validation, clinical validation, legal opinion, or evidence that the software is accurate for other veterans. This public release includes limited, reproducible tests using fictional case data. Automated checks of a script or prompt structure cannot establish an AI system's accuracy when analyzing real records, complying with law, or resisting hallucinations. The planned AI-behavior test scenarios are not a set of passing evaluations. **Independent review by qualified medical reviewers and VA-accredited representatives is still required.** See `PUBLIC_VALIDATION_REPORT.md` for exactly what has and has not been tested.

## Supports a VSO — does not replace one

This project is designed to help veterans:
- Find and organize existing documented medical and service evidence.
- Understand applicable official VA rules and what still needs verification.
- Prepare evidence-focused questions for a free VA-accredited Veterans Service Organization (VSO) representative.

**It does not replace an accredited VSO**, VA-accredited claims agent, attorney, licensed clinician, C&P examiner, or the VA claims-adjudication process. It cannot create a valid medical diagnosis, provide a medical nexus opinion, determine service connection or entitlements, file claims automatically, or guarantee a disability percentage, payment, effective date, or successful appeal. Its outputs are informational and may be incomplete or wrong. Do not rely on them alone for medical treatment, appeal deadlines, or claims decisions.

Official help: https://www.va.gov/get-help-from-accredited-representative/

## Privacy and security

Do not upload or commit real VA or other medical records to this repository, public issues, pull requests, discussions, or CI. Before using a third-party AI product, review its privacy, retention, access-control, and training policies. This package does not certify that an external model or deployment is HIPAA-compliant or secure. See `SECURITY.md`.

## Current rules

VA disability laws and rating criteria change. Verify controlling rules against official, relevant-date law; public examples and earlier decisions do not establish a particular veteran's eligibility. This project has no authority to interpret binding law for an individual.
